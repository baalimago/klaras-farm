Tap and hear is the game for a 1-year-old: tap anything and it jumps while Mum's recorded voice says its name (shown here as the bubble). Find it is the later level: the bubble asks for one thing and the right tap gets confetti. Fish and jellyfish swim, the crab scuttles sideways, the spoon and teapot hop, and the chair walks on its four legs.

How parents build a place

1. Draw the scene

A few big strokes: a barn, a window, some seaweed. The app turns them into felt.

2. Draw a friend

One outline per thing. Gaps get closed automatically. Klara can stamp the eyes on.

3. Say its name

Record "spoon" in your language. Each parent can record in their own language.

4. Pick how it moves

One tap. No skeleton to place. Then it pops into the scene.
