Six parent flows, drawn as iPad screens in the Sunny felt look. Every flow starts in edit mode. Parent screens use English here as a placeholder; the parent UI language is still open.

Audio feedback used throughout (iPads have no vibration)

  • Holding: a soft tone rises with the felt ring
  • Hold complete: a "pop"
  • Enter edit mode: three rising notes; leave: three falling notes
  • Record starts: a short blip; stops: a lower blip
  • Saved: a felt "plink" as the thing drops in
  • Delete: a soft "poof", only after confirming
